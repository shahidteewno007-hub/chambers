    The Exam Hall Act, 2026

    Act No. VIII of 2026

    An Act to help Batch 14 walk into every paper calm, prepared and on time.

    Whereas it is expedient that no student shall panic; it is hereby enacted as follows:

    1. Read before you write. Spend the first five minutes reading every question. Tick the ones you know best and start with those.
    2. Time follows marks. Split your time by the marks each question carries, and keep the last ten minutes for checking.
    3. Headings are mandatory. Examiners skim. Clear headings, short paragraphs and underlined key terms help them find your points.
    4. Cite like counsel. Name the section, the article or the case, with its year, wherever you can.
    5. Open and close. Begin with a one-line definition. End with a line that answers the exact question asked.
    6. Sleep is not an offence. A rested brain remembers more than a tired one. Do the Last-Minute Read, then go to bed.
    7. Saving clause. If your mind goes blank, write the definition, the relevant section and one example. Partial marks are still marks.

    Given under the seal of the Chambers, November 2026.
